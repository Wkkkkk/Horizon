## [Samsung to Double Production of HBM4 and HBM4E DRAM](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/20/samsung-to-double-hbm4-output-next-year-sources-say) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Samsung plans to more than double its production of HBM4 and HBM4E DRAM by next year. This expansion is expected to significantly impact the supply of high-performance memory for AI accelerators and consumer electronics. The increased production of HBM4 and HBM4E DRAM by Samsung could alleviate current supply constraints in the semiconductor industry, particularly for AI accelerators which are in high demand. This move may also influence the pricing and availability of consumer electronics that rely on these advanced memory technologies. HBM4 and HBM4E are high bandwidth memory technologies that offer significant improvements in data transfer rates and efficiency over traditional DRAM. Samsung's expansion in production capacity is likely to support the growing demand from AI and high-performance computing sectors.

hackernews · giuliomagnifico · Sep 20, 17:38 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49778029)

**Background**: High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is a type of memory interface for 3D-stacked DRAM, which provides higher bandwidth and efficiency compared to traditional DRAM. It is widely used in graphics accelerators, AI processors, and high-performance computing. The HBM4 standard was officially announced in April 2025, and it represents the latest evolution in this technology, offering improved performance metrics. Samsung, along with other major manufacturers like SK Hynix and Micron, plays a significant role in the production of HBM.
