## [Exploring the Holographic Nature of Gravity and Reality](https://www.quantamagazine.org/gravity-seems-holographic-what-does-that-mean-for-reality-20260925/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The article discusses the holographic principle in physics, suggesting that gravity and the universe might be understood as holographic. This concept has sparked significant debate and discussion within the scientific community. Understanding gravity as holographic could revolutionize our comprehension of the universe, impacting theories in quantum gravity and cosmology. It challenges traditional views and could lead to new insights into the fundamental nature of reality. The holographic principle suggests that a three-dimensional universe can be described by information encoded on a two-dimensional boundary. This principle is rooted in string theory and has implications for resolving the black hole information paradox.

hackernews · ibobev · Sep 25, 15:31 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49845998)

**Background**: The holographic principle is a concept in theoretical physics that proposes a lower-dimensional boundary can encode all the information of a higher-dimensional space. First proposed by Gerard 't Hooft and later developed by Leonard Susskind, it is a key idea in string theory. The principle is often illustrated by the AdS/CFT correspondence, which provides a duality between a gravity theory in a higher-dimensional space and a quantum field theory on its boundary. This principle has been influential in addressing the black hole information paradox.
